# Xianxia v0.1.7 — Artifact Forging, Cultivation, and New Techniques

Released July 28, 2026.

This update introduces the Artifact Forging Path, new crafting stations, spiritual artifacts, renewed spiritual plants, a new technique, an independent cultivator merchant, and numerous fixes.

## Artifact Forging

- Added an independent five-Tier Artifact Forging Path with Low, Middle, and High Stages.
- Progress by crafting spiritual artifacts; Tier, Stage, and EXP are saved and synchronized in multiplayer.
- Added the Artifact Forge, Spirit Jade Forge, and Profound Forge with distinct textures and updated inventory and manual icons.
- Added Crude, Common, Refined, Earth Grade, and Heaven Grade artifact quality.
- Quality modifies artifact power, Qi cost, and forging EXP.
- Lower-Tier artifacts grant significantly less EXP as the player's forging Tier increases.
- Tooltips now display exact bonuses, effective Qi costs, quality, base EXP, and the current forging reward.

## New Artifacts

- Verdant Antler Staff — fires a homing green projectile that inflicts poison.
- Jade Antler Talisman — increases defense, regeneration, and magic damage.
- Flame Spirit Fan — launches three Qi fireballs.
- Thunderclap Seal — unleashes a long-range electrical attack.
- Beast Soul Banner — summons a spiritual guardian and improves summon damage, magic damage, defense, and minion capacity.
- Improved the Beast Soul Guardian during fast movement, teleportation, combat, and owner recall.

## Spiritual Rain and Plants

- Added Spiritual Rain, a Qi Gathering technique that advances nearby immature spiritual plants by one growth stage.
- Spiritual Rain initially costs 40 Qi and has a 15-second cooldown; mastery reduces its cost and increases its radius.
- Added new textures for all four seed types and every plant growth stage.
- Increased plant visibility and fixed a temporary split-sprite issue.
- Naturally generated spiritual plants now have a 2.5% seed drop chance.
- Mature spiritual plants always drop herbs and new seeds.

## Alchemy and Independent Cultivation

- Added coordinated textures for all three Alchemy Cauldron Tiers.
- Fixed placement cases that could cause Cauldrons or Artifact Forges to disappear.
- Added Wanderer Shen, an independent cultivator merchant who arrives after the player reaches Qi Gathering.
- Wanderer Shen trades selected technique manuals for Spirit Stones, allowing players to remain independent from Sects.

## Configuration, Interface, and Fixes

- Added a server setting that scales Qi EXP requirements from 1x to 10x.
- Existing characters retain their Stage and progression percentage when the multiplier changes.
- Fixed tooltips displaying `\n` instead of starting a new line.
- Improved multiplayer synchronization for Paths and artifacts.
- Updated the Cultivator's Manual with new pages and recipes.
- Updated English, Italian, and Simplified Chinese localizations.
- Added numerous fixes for graphics, object placement, and NPC behavior.
