[h1]Xianxia[/h1]

Cultivate Qi and walk the path to immortality in Terraria.

[b]Begin as a mortal, master spiritual techniques, join a sect, forge artifacts, build formations, and defy Heavenly Tribulations to reach the Nascent Soul Realm.[/b]

[h2]Cultivation[/h2]
[list]
[*]5 Realms and 45 total cultivation stages.[/*]
[*]Qi fuels techniques while QiEXP preserves permanent progress.[/*]
[*]Realm breakthroughs have success chances influenced by Spiritual Roots, pills, Heart Demons, and breakthrough quality.[/*]
[*]Choose Inferior, Stable, Perfect, or Heavenly Foundation, then forge a Golden Core from Tier 9 to Tier 1.[/*]
[*]Face stronger Heavenly Tribulations based on Realm, Foundation quality, and Golden Core Tier.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Spiritual Roots and Techniques[/h2]
[list]
[*]Spiritual Roots provide elemental affinities, meditation bonuses, biome resonance, and breakthrough modifiers.[/*]
[*]Technique Loadouts include fixed slots, three presets, a synchronized Ability Wheel, and optional direct hotkeys.[/*]
[*]Cast Fireball, Qi Palm, Flame Step, Spiritual Rain, Spirit Sword Rain, Void Step, and more.[/*]
[*]Toggle Qi Sense, Qi Protection, Qi Burning, Night Vision, Qi Flight, Spiritual Pressure, and Sect Formation.[/*]
[*]Techniques gain EXP through use and become stronger with mastery.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Exploration and Resources[/h2]
[list]
[*]Spirit Stone mines, Spirit Jade, Profound Iron, Spirit Crystals, and Spirit Vein Compasses.[/*]
[*]Qi Sense highlights spiritual ores and improves with mastery.[/*]
[*]Existing worlds can gain Spirit Jade and Profound Iron through progression events and NPC access.[/*]
[*]Spiritual zones and elemental biomes improve meditation.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Alchemy and Artifact Forging[/h2]
[list]
[*]Independent Alchemy and Artifact Forging Paths with Realm-based Tiers and mastery progression.[/*]
[*]Three Cauldrons, three Artifact Forges, renewable spiritual herbs, pills, weapons, accessories, and artifacts.[/*]
[*]Persistent item quality from Impure or Crude up to Heaven Grade.[/*]
[*]Pills support cultivation, combat, cleansing, breakthroughs, and Tribulation preparation.[/*]
[*]Heavenly Treasures from bosses can empower breakthroughs and grant permanent bonuses.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Spirit Beasts and Verdant Cloud Sect[/h2]
[list]
[*]Four animated Spirit Beasts with Realms, stages, behaviors, drops, and biome-based spawning.[/*]
[*]Join Elder Jian's sect, complete missions, earn contribution, rise through ranks, and buy manuals.[/*]
[*]Learn Sword Intent, Spirit Sword Rain, and Sect Protection Formation.[/*]
[*]Independent cultivators can trade with Wanderer Shen.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Permanent Array Formations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Place persistent Formation Cores with stored Qi, integrity, ownership, permissions, and upgrades.[/*]
[*]Use Protection, Spirit Gathering, Suppression, and Restoration modes.[/*]
[*]Relay Flags extend territory and share Qi and integrity across the network.[/*]
[*]Spirit Veins and Spirit Stone mines can power nearby formations.[/*]
[/list]
