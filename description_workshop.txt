[h1]Xianxia[/h1]

Cultivate Qi and walk the path to immortality in Terraria.

[b]Begin as a mere mortal, master spiritual arts, build powerful formations, join a sect, and defy the heavens to reach the Nascent Soul Realm.[/b]

[h2]Cultivation[/h2]
[list]
[*]5 Realms: Mortal, Qi Gathering, Foundation Establishment, Core Formation, and Nascent Soul.[/*]
[*]9 Stages in every Realm, for a total of 45 cultivation levels.[/*]
[*]Breakthroughs grant increasingly powerful stats at higher cultivation levels.[/*]
[*]Qi is spendable while QiEXP permanently preserves your cultivation progress.[/*]
[*]Configurable hold/toggle meditation, Realm-scaled recovery, Qi Concentration bonuses, and multiplayer time acceleration.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Techniques and Abilities[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ability Wheel and Realm-based menu with icons, requirements, active techniques, and passives.[/*]
[*]Every technique earns EXP through use and becomes stronger as it levels.[/*]
[*]Meditation, Spiritual Breathing, Qi Sense, Qi Resistance, Qi Protection, and Night Vision.[/*]
[*]Fireball, Qi Palm, Flame Step, Qi Flight, Void Step, and Spiritual Pressure.[/*]
[*]Golden Core Circulation, Nascent Soul Regeneration, Sword Intent, Spirit Sword Rain, and Sect Protection Formation.[/*]
[*]Use Spiritual Rain to accelerate the growth of nearby spiritual plants.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Breakthroughs and Heavenly Tribulations[/h2]
[list]
[*]From Core Formation onward, Realm breakthroughs require a confirmed Heavenly Tribulation.[/*]
[*]Survive 9 lightning strikes by default; higher Realms inflict greater damage and Qi pressure.[/*]
[*]Lightning partially penetrates Qi Protection, making equipment, pills, and preparation important.[/*]
[*]Cancelling preserves QiEXP, while failing the Tribulation prevents advancement.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Exploration and Spiritual Resources[/h2]
[list]
[*]Small, medium, and large natural Spirit Stone mines generated in the Cavern layer.[/*]
[*]Spirit Stones are finite mining resources and cannot be crafted.[/*]
[*]Spirit Stone mines radiate Qi and can power nearby permanent Formation networks.[/*]
[*]Three Spirit Vein Compasses plus Spirit Crystals, Spirit Jade, and Profound Iron.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Spirit Beasts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Four animated cultivation creatures: Spirit Hare, Jade Horn Deer, Flame-Tailed Fox, and Thunderclaw Tiger.[/*]
[*]Each has a Realm, Stage, behavior, danger label, scaled stats, and unique rewards.[/*]
[*]Stronger beasts appear farther from world spawn across forests, snow, jungles, and beaches.[/*]
[*]Beast materials support Alchemy and Formation upgrades; spawning and scaling are configurable.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Alchemy[/h2]
[list]
[*]A complete Alchemy Path with 5 Realm-based Tiers and Low, Middle, and High Stages.[/*]
[*]Three refining stations: Alchemy Cauldron, Spirit Jade Cauldron, and Profound Alchemy Cauldron.[/*]
[*]Renewable Spirit Grass, Fire Lotus, Moon Dew Flower, and Ironroot with visible growth stages.[/*]
[*]Unique seed and growth-stage artwork makes every spiritual plant easy to identify.[/*]
[*]Qi, cultivation, combat, cleansing, Tribulation, and Spirit Beast pills.[/*]
[*]Every crafted pill receives a persistent quality: Impure, Common, Refined, Earth Grade, or Heaven Grade.[/*]
[*]Pill quality changes effect strength, duration, cleansing power, and Saturation cost.[/*]
[*]Mastery and better Cauldrons improve yield and quality; Saturation prevents unlimited stacking.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Artifact Forging[/h2]
[list]
[*]A complete independent Artifact Forging Path with 5 Tiers and Low, Middle, and High Stages.[/*]
[*]Forge spiritual artifacts to gain Path EXP; higher-quality creations grant significantly more progress.[/*]
[*]Three distinct crafting stations: Artifact Forge, Spirit Jade Forge, and Profound Forge.[/*]
[*]Every artifact receives a persistent quality: Crude, Common, Refined, Earth Grade, or Heaven Grade.[/*]
[*]Quality modifies artifact power, Qi consumption, and the Artifact Forging EXP earned when it is created.[/*]
[*]Artifact tooltips display exact bonuses, effective Qi costs, quality, and forging rewards.[/*]
[*]Create the Verdant Antler Staff, Jade Antler Talisman, Flame Spirit Fan, Thunderclap Seal, and Beast Soul Banner.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Verdant Cloud Sect[/h2]
[list]
[*]Join the Verdant Cloud Sect through Elder Jian after reaching Qi Gathering.[/*]
[*]Advance from Outer Disciple to Inner Disciple, Core Disciple, and Sect Elder.[/*]
[*]Complete missions and earn spendable Tokens plus permanent lifetime contribution.[/*]
[*]Purchase spiritual supplies and manuals for Sword Intent, Spirit Sword Rain, and Sect Protection Formation.[/*]
[*]Sustain the Sect Formation with personal or allied Qi, Spirit Stones, or a Spirit Vein.[/*]
[*]Players who prefer to remain independent can trade Spirit Stones with Wanderer Shen for selected techniques.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Permanent Array Formations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Train the Array Formation Path through combat, absorbed damage, Spirit Vein links, and Tribulations.[/*]
[*]Place persistent Formation Cores with stored Qi, integrity, ownership, access rules, and a dedicated UI.[/*]
[*]Manually upgrade structural Stages and Tiers using materials and Beast Cores.[/*]
[*]Enable multiple Formation types: Protection, Spirit Gathering, Suppression, and Restoration.[/*]
[*]Protection blocks projectiles, repels enemies, prevents hostile spawning, and intercepts Tribulations.[/*]
[*]Integrity repairs with network Qi; nearby Spirit Stone mines provide limited automatic power.[/*]
[*]Core radius reaches 80 blocks; Relay Flags add 40-block territories sharing Qi and integrity.[/*]
[*]Use Normal Extension for all Core modes, or choose one amplified Relay specialization.[/*]
[*]Specialized Relay modes provide stronger protection, x2.25 Qi recovery, heavier enemy suppression, or +20 life regeneration.[/*]
[/list]
