[h1]Xianxia[/h1]

Cultivate Qi and walk the path to immortality in Terraria.

[b]Begin as a mere mortal, master the spiritual arts, and defy the heavens to reach the Nascent Soul Realm.[/b]

[h2]Cultivation[/h2]
[list]
[*]5 Realms: Mortal, Qi Gathering, Foundation Establishment, Core Formation, and Nascent Soul.[/*]
[*]9 Stages in every Realm, for a total of 45 cultivation levels.[/*]
[*]Stats, regeneration, and flight speed that grow dramatically in higher Realms.[/*]
[*]Separate Qi and QiEXP systems: spending Qi never causes you to lose progress, Stages, or Realms.[/*]
[*]Meditation with a configurable keybind, Realm-scaling passive Qi recovery, and accelerated Qi restoration when below QiEXP.[/*]
[*]Qi Concentration levels from 0 to 10 around Spirit Stone mines, greatly strengthening meditation and passive recovery.[/*]
[*]Configurable time acceleration while meditating, with multiplayer support.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Techniques and Abilities[/h2]
[list]
[*]An Ability Wheel for quickly activating techniques and passive abilities.[/*]
[*]A Realm-based Ability Menu displaying icons, requirements, and progression.[/*]
[*]Every technique earns EXP when used and becomes stronger as it levels up.[/*]
[*]At least one Realm-specific passive ability at every step of the cultivation path.[/*]
[*]Meditation, Spiritual Breathing, Qi Sense, and Qi Resistance.[/*]
[*]Fireball, Qi Palm, and Flame Step.[/*]
[*]Qi Protection, Night Vision, Golden Core Circulation, and Qi Flight.[/*]
[*]Nascent Soul Regeneration, Void Step, and Spiritual Pressure.[/*]
[*]Technique items that let you cast Fireball and Qi Palm directly from your inventory.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Breakthroughs and Heavenly Tribulations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Visual effects that grow more powerful with every Stage and Realm breakthrough.[/*]
[*]Starting at Core Formation, every Realm breakthrough requires a Heavenly Tribulation.[/*]
[*]Confirm before beginning and survive 9 lightning strikes by default, with harsher Tribulations in higher Realms.[/*]
[*]Tribulation lightning drains a percentage of maximum Qi and partially penetrates Qi Protection, making preparation essential.[/*]
[*]Failing a Tribulation prevents the Realm advancement.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Exploration and Spiritual Resources[/h2]
[list]
[*]Small, medium, and large natural Spirit Stone mines generated in the Cavern layer.[/*]
[*]Spirit Stones are finite mining resources and cannot be crafted.[/*]
[*]Spirit Stone mines radiate Qi, strengthening meditation and passive recovery nearby.[/*]
[*]A Spirit Vein Compass with two upgrades that increase its detection range.[/*]
[*]Custom ores: Spirit Jade and Profound Iron.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Spirit Beasts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Four cultivation creatures with custom animated sprites, behaviors, and Realm-scaled Stages.[/*]
[*]Spirit Hare, Jade Horn Deer, Flame-Tailed Fox, and the elite Thunderclaw Tiger.[/*]
[*]Readable world labels show each beast's name, Realm, Stage, and danger relative to your cultivation.[/*]
[*]Stronger Beast Realms and higher Stages appear progressively farther from the shared world spawn.[/*]
[*]Spirit Beasts naturally inhabit forests, snow biomes, jungles, and beaches.[/*]
[*]Beast life, damage, defense, rewards, and materials scale with cultivation.[/*]
[*]New Beast Cores, elemental essences, fur, and blood for advanced alchemy.[/*]
[*]Natural spawning, distance scaling, and spawn rate are configurable by the server.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Items, Equipment, and Alchemy[/h2]
[list]
[*]A complete Spirit Jade armor set.[/*]
[*]Spirit Jade Sword, Profound Iron Spear, and a homing Flying Sword.[/*]
[*]A ring, pendant, and talisman with cultivation-related bonuses.[/*]
[*]An Alchemy Cauldron and the Alchemy Path.[/*]
[*]Five Alchemy Tiers matching Mortal through Nascent Soul, each with Low, Middle, and High Stages.[/*]
[*]Spirit Grass, Fire Lotus, Moon Dew Flower, and Ironroot: renewable herbs obtainable in existing worlds.[/*]
[*]Three refining tiers: Alchemy Cauldron, Spirit Jade Cauldron, and Profound Alchemy Cauldron.[/*]
[*]Higher Mastery and better Cauldrons improve bonus yield and reduce impurities.[/*]
[*]Pill Saturation prevents unlimited stacking and gradually reduces pill effectiveness.[/*]
[*]Pills that restore flat or Realm-scaling Qi, cleanse saturation, or provide temporary cultivation and combat bonuses.[/*]
[*]Four Spirit Beast pills for body tempering, flame meridians, thunder resistance, and core refinement.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Quality of Life and Configuration[/h2]
[list]
[*]A Cultivator's Manual received upon first entering a world, featuring an interactive index, explanations, and illustrated recipes.[/*]
[*]Scrollable Manual pages with mouse-wheel, draggable scrollbar, return-to-top control, and remembered page.[/*]
[*]Hover over the Qi bar for next-Stage requirements, stat gains, ability unlocks, and Tribulation preparation estimates.[/*]
[*]Client options for Qi bar position and scale, Qi Concentration visibility, and visual effect intensity.[/*]
[*]Commands for inspecting cultivation progress and controlling certain passive abilities.[/*]
[*]Optional debug commands for testing Realms, Stages, abilities, effects, Heavenly Tribulations, and Spirit Beasts.[/*]
[*]Use [b]/xiadebug spawncheck[/b] to diagnose Spirit Beast spawning in the current area.[/*]
[*]Fully configurable keybinds.[/*]
[*]Ability terrain destruction can be disabled globally or separately for each technique.[/*]
[*]Full English and Italian localization.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] To discover Spirit Stone mines, explore a world created with the mod enabled.
