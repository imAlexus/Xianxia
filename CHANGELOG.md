# Changelog

All notable Xianxia releases are documented here.

## [0.1.4] - 2026-07-22

### Alchemy Expansion and Chinese Localization

- Added five Alchemy Tiers, from Mortal through Nascent Soul, with Low, Middle, and High Stages.
- Added Spirit Grass, Fire Lotus, Moon Dew Flower, and Ironroot as renewable spiritual herbs.
- Added Spirit Jade and Profound Alchemy Cauldrons.
- Added Alchemy Mastery, bonus yields, impurities, and Pill Saturation.
- Added new progression, recovery, breakthrough, resistance, and utility pills.
- Added Simplified Chinese localization.
- Expanded the Cultivator's Manual and Workshop documentation.

## [0.1.3] - 2026-07-22

### Spirit Beasts

- Added the Spirit Hare, Jade Horn Deer, Flame-Tailed Fox, and Thunderclaw Tiger.
- Added distance-based Realm and Stage progression for Spirit Beasts.
- Added Beast Cores, Spirit Fur, Spirit Beast Blood, elemental essences, and Spirit Beast pills.
- Added configurable natural spawning and diagnostic commands.
- Improved Spirit Beast labels and fixed Qi Flight being interrupted by chat.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-07-21

### Interface and Quality of Life

- Added breakthrough readiness information to the Qi bar tooltip.
- Added scrolling, a draggable scrollbar, return-to-top navigation, and remembered pages to the Cultivator's Manual.
- Added configurable Qi bar position and scale.
- Added Qi Concentration visibility and visual-effect intensity options.
- Reduced the packaged mod size and improved interface presentation.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-07-19

### Cultivation Balance

- Increased Heavenly Tribulation difficulty and introduced percentage-based Qi damage.
- Improved Qi Protection against environmental damage and debuffs.
- Expanded Qi Concentration from level 0 to 10.
- Improved Spirit Stone Mine cultivation bonuses.
- Added Night Vision and improved Spiritual Pressure.
- Expanded debug commands, balance, documentation, and bug fixes.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-19

### Initial Release

- Introduced five cultivation Realms and 45 total Stages.
- Added Qi, QiEXP, meditation, breakthrough progression, and Heavenly Tribulations.
- Added cultivation techniques, passive abilities, and the Ability Wheel.
- Added Spirit Stone mines, spiritual equipment, basic alchemy, and the Cultivator's Manual.
- Added English and Italian localization.
