[h1]Xianxia 0.1.8 - Spiritual Roots[/h1]

[b]Workshop hotfix build: 0.1.8.2[/b]

This update introduces Spiritual Roots, breakthrough grades, Technique Loadouts, Qi Burning, Heart Demons, improved spiritual ore access, and major interface upgrades.

[h2]Spiritual Roots[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added permanent Mixed, True, Heavenly, Mutated, and Primordial Spiritual Roots.
[*]Roots contain elemental affinities, purity, a primary element, and meditation bonuses.
[*]Elements improve compatible technique power, Qi cost, mastery gain, artifacts, and passive statistics.
[*]Added biome resonance: Wood/Jungle, Fire/Underworld, Earth/Underground, Metal/Dungeon, Water/Ocean, Lightning/Rain, Ice/Snow, Wind/Sky, and Void/Space.
[*]Roots can be appraised by Elder Jian or Wanderer Shen and are synchronized in multiplayer.
[/list]

[h2]Technique Loadouts and Ability Wheel[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added three persistent, multiplayer-synchronized Technique Loadout presets.
[*]Available slots increase from 2 at Mortal to 6 at Nascent Soul.
[*]Foundation Establishment unlocks presets 2 and 3.
[*]Equip unlocked cast techniques through the Abilities page.
[*]The Ability Wheel mirrors the active preset. Select a technique, then cast it with the universal key.
[*]Optional individual hotkeys remain available and work without equipping the technique.
[*]Added a persistent Toggle semicircle for Qi Sense, Qi Protection, Qi Burning, Night Vision, Qi Flight, Spiritual Pressure, and Sect Protection Formation.
[*]Spiritual Rain and Spirit Sword Rain are now compatible with loadout slots.
[*]Added scrolling to the Ability Tree and improved cards, progress bars, elemental colors, details, and layout.
[/list]

[h2]Realm Breakthroughs[/h2]
[list]
[*]Realm breakthroughs now use visible success chances that decrease at higher Realms.
[*]Spiritual Roots, pills, Heart Demons, and selected breakthrough grade modify success chance.
[*]Added centered confirmation UI with chance breakdown and catalyst slots.
[*]Added Inferior, Stable, Perfect, and Heavenly Foundation qualities.
[*]Higher Foundation quality grants stronger statistics and faster Qi gathering.
[*]Golden Core now has nine Tiers: Tier 9 is default and Tier 1 is strongest.
[*]Higher Golden Core Tiers grant better statistics and Qi gathering but reduce breakthrough chance.
[*]Tribulation strength scales with Realm, Foundation quality, and Golden Core Tier.
[*]Added breakthrough pills for Qi Gathering, Foundation, Golden Core, and Nascent Soul.
[/list]

[h2]Heavenly Treasures[/h2]
[list]
[*]Foundation requires one Foundation Pill or one Heavenly Treasure; higher Foundation grades have stricter requirements.
[*]Golden Core requires one chosen Heavenly Treasure; Tier 1 also requires a Golden Core Pill.
[*]Eye of Cthulhu, Queen Bee, and Skeletron guarantee unique Heavenly Treasures.
[*]Successful use grants permanent critical chance/Qi Sense, life/regeneration, or defense/damage reduction bonuses.
[*]Added Terraria-scale textures, multiline tooltips, catalyst selection, and permanent records in the Character tab.
[/list]

[h2]Qi Burning and Heart Demons[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added Qi Burning: sacrifice maximum Qi capacity for major combat bonuses.
[*]Burned capacity persists, blocks Realm advancement, and causes Qi Deviation when Qi Burning ends.
[*]Repair the cultivation origin through meditation or Meridian Mending Pills.
[*]Repair meditation restores current Qi without increasing QiEXP until the origin is healed.
[*]Added Heart Demon points from eligible deaths and failed Realm breakthroughs.
[*]Heart Demons reduce cultivation gain and breakthrough chance.
[*]Added personal Heart Demon trials with a Realm-scaled boss and server-authoritative multiplayer state.
[*]Fixed death and breakthrough-failure counters in the Character UI and multiplayer.
[/list]

[h2]Spiritual Ores and Existing Worlds[/h2]
[list]
[*]Greatly increased Spirit Jade and Profound Iron generation; Spirit Stone generation is unchanged.
[*]Existing worlds receive a one-time safe migration for missing Spirit Jade and Profound Iron.
[*]Qi Sense now highlights both ores through walls with distinct pulsing colors.
[*]Wanderer Shen and Elder Jian sell progression-appropriate Spirit Jade and Profound Iron.
[*]Fixed higher Artifact Forges so they can craft recipes from all lower Forge Tiers.
[/list]

[h2]Character and Interface[/h2]
[list]
[*]Expanded Character tab with Spiritual Root, affinities, Realm grade, Golden Core Tier, combat statistics, Qi bonuses, catalysts, breakthrough history, and Heart Demon progress.
[*]Improved text size, multiline descriptions, catalyst records, and long-stat readability.
[*]Breakthrough readiness now displays correct current, effective, base, and burned Qi values.
[*]Added the Damaged Cultivation Origin status with repair instructions.
[*]Fixed repeated breakthrough prompts interrupting meditation while the cultivation origin is damaged.
[/list]

[h2]Other Improvements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Updated Cultivator Manual and English, Italian, and Simplified Chinese localization.
[*]Added server settings for Qi Burning, burned Qi limits, Heart Demons, and penalty strength.
[*]Improved Ability Wheel title placement and preserved Toggle-menu state after closing the wheel.
[*]Shortened the Workshop description to remain safely below Steam limits.
[*]Updated RIASSUNTO_MOD.md with the complete 0.1.8.2 systems, file counts, and changelog summary.
[*]Aligned ROADMAP_0.2.0.md with current version 0.1.8.2.
[*]Verified compilation and packaged build with 0 errors.
[/list]
