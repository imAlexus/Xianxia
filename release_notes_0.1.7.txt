[h1]Xianxia 0.1.7 - Artifact Forging, Cultivation, and New Techniques[/h1]

Version 0.1.7 introduces the new Artifact Forging Path, new crafting stations, spiritual artifacts, crops, techniques, and numerous fixes.

[h2]Artifact Forging[/h2]

[list]
[*]Added a new independent Path with 5 Tiers.
[*]Each Tier contains Low, Middle, and High Stages.
[*]Progress by crafting spiritual artifacts.
[*]Tier, Stage, and EXP are saved and synchronized in multiplayer.
[*]Added a dedicated page to the Cultivation > Paths menu.
[/list]

[h2]Artifact Quality and EXP[/h2]

Every artifact receives an individual quality grade that modifies its power, Qi consumption, and Artifact Forging EXP:

[list]
[*]Crude: 82% power, 120% Qi cost, 50% EXP.
[*]Common: 100% power, 100% Qi cost, 100% EXP.
[*]Refined: 112% power, 92% Qi cost, 200% EXP.
[*]Earth Grade: 127% power, 82% Qi cost, 500% EXP.
[*]Heaven Grade: 148% power, 70% Qi cost, 1000% EXP.
[/list]

Artifacts from Tiers below your current Path Tier now grant significantly less EXP. Tooltips now display quality, exact stats, effective Qi cost, base EXP, and the current EXP reward.

[h2]New Forges[/h2]

[list]
[*]Artifact Forge.
[*]Spirit Jade Forge.
[*]Profound Forge.
[*]New and distinct textures for each Tier.
[*]Fixed cases where forges could disappear after being placed.
[*]Updated inventory and manual icons.
[/list]

[h2]New Artifacts[/h2]

[list]
[*]Verdant Antler Staff - fires a homing green projectile that inflicts poison.
[*]Jade Antler Talisman - increases defense, regeneration, and magic damage.
[*]Flame Spirit Fan - launches three Qi fireballs.
[*]Thunderclap Seal - unleashes a long-range electrical attack.
[*]Beast Soul Banner - summons a spiritual guardian and improves summon damage, magic damage, defense, and minion capacity.
[/list]

Improved the Beast Soul Guardian's behavior during fast movement, teleportation, combat, and when returning to its owner.

[h2]Spiritual Rain[/h2]

Added the new Spiritual Rain Technique:

[list]
[*]Advances every immature spiritual plant in the area by one growth stage.
[*]Requires the Qi Gathering Realm.
[*]Initially costs 40 Qi.
[*]Mastery reduces the cost and increases the radius.
[*]Has a 15-second cooldown.
[*]Added to the Abilities menu with a new icon.
[/list]

[h2]Spiritual Plants[/h2]

[list]
[*]New textures for all four seed types.
[*]New textures for all four growth stages.
[*]Plants are now larger and easier to see.
[*]Fixed a visual issue that could temporarily split a plant into two.
[*]Seeds obtained from naturally generated plants now have a 2.5% drop chance.
[*]Mature spiritual plants always drop herbs and new seeds.
[/list]

[h2]Alchemy[/h2]

[list]
[*]New matching textures for all three cauldron Tiers.
[*]Updated inventory and manual icons.
[*]Fixed placement issues that could cause cauldrons to disappear.
[/list]

[h2]Independent Cultivator Merchant[/h2]

Added Wanderer Shen, a new town NPC for players who do not wish to join a Sect.

[list]
[*]Arrives after reaching the Qi Gathering Realm.
[*]Trades techniques in exchange for Spirit Stones.
[*]Sword Intent: 40 Spirit Stones.
[*]Spiritual Sword Rain: 120 Spirit Stones.
[*]Protective Formation: 300 Spirit Stones.
[*]Each technique retains a minimum Realm requirement.
[*]New animated texture, icon, dialogue, and Bestiary entry.
[/list]

[h2]Difficulty Configuration[/h2]

Added a new server configuration setting that multiplies the Qi EXP required for every Stage and Realm:

[list]
[*]1x preserves the original progression.
[*]2x doubles all requirements.
[*]Configurable up to 10x.
[*]Existing characters retain their Stage and progression percentage when the setting changes.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes and Improvements[/h2]

[list]
[*]Fixed tooltips displaying "\n" instead of starting a new line.
[*]Artifact tooltips now display exact stats and Qi costs.
[*]Improved multiplayer synchronization for Paths and artifacts.
[*]Updated the Cultivator's Manual with new pages and recipes.
[*]Updated English, Italian, and Simplified Chinese localizations.
[*]Numerous fixes for graphics, object placement, and NPC behavior.
[/list]
