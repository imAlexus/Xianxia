[h1]Xianxia v0.1.6 - Sects and Permanent Formations[/h1]

This update introduces the Verdant Cloud Sect, pill quality, and a complete persistent Array Formation system.

[h2]Verdant Cloud Sect[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added Elder Jian, who can join a suitable house and accepts cultivators from Qi Gathering onward.[/*]
[*]Added four Sect ranks: Outer Disciple, Inner Disciple, Core Disciple, and Sect Elder.[/*]
[*]Added hunting, delivery, Alchemy, Spirit Vein exploration, and Heavenly Tribulation missions.[/*]
[*]Added Sect Contribution Tokens, lifetime contribution, a dedicated Sect menu, and the Sect shop.[/*]
[*]Added Sword Intent, Spirit Sword Rain, and Sect Protection Formation manuals.[/*]
[*]Improved Spirit Sword Rain so five swords launch from the cultivator toward the cursor.[/*]
[*]Expanded Sect Protection Formation with shared Qi support, allied meditation contributions, automatic Spirit Stone use, Spirit Vein power, scaling integrity, and longer duration.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Permanent Array Formations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added the Array Formation Path with five Tiers and Low, Middle, and High Stages.[/*]
[*]Path EXP is earned through real Formation use: maintaining territory during combat, absorbing damage, linking Spirit Veins, and intercepting Tribulations.[/*]
[*]Added placeable Permanent Formation Cores with persistent ownership, stored Qi, integrity, access rules, world resource bars, and a dedicated control UI.[/*]
[*]Added Protection, Spirit Gathering, Suppression, and Restoration modes. Higher structural Tiers can operate multiple modes together.[/*]
[*]Protection barriers destroy hostile projectiles entering from outside, lose integrity from impacts, repel enemies, prevent hostile natural spawning, and intercept Heavenly Tribulation lightning.[/*]
[*]Formation integrity automatically repairs by consuming network Qi.[/*]
[*]Core upgrades are now manual. Every structural Stage and Tier consumes materials, requires sufficient Array Formation mastery, and improves Formation statistics.[/*]
[*]Spirit Stone mines can automatically power nearby Cores and Relay Flags, with generation limited by local Qi Concentration.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Formation Relay Flags[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added Relay Flags that extend a Core network with separate 40-block territories beyond the Core's maximum 80-block radius.[/*]
[*]Relays share the Core's Qi and integrity, have their own upkeep, display world bars, and can channel distant Spirit Veins.[/*]
[*]Added a Relay status UI showing its parent Core, link distance, upkeep, shared resources, and local spiritual concentration.[/*]
[*]Normal Extension carries every enabled Core mode into Relay territory at standard strength.[/*]
[*]Relays can instead specialize in Protection, Spirit Gathering, Suppression, or Restoration for stronger local effects.[/*]
[*]Relay limits, link range, choices, and network state are saved and synchronized in multiplayer.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Alchemy and Pill Quality[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every crafted pill now receives a persistent quality: Impure, Common, Refined, Earth Grade, or Heaven Grade.[/*]
[*]Quality changes pill strength, buff duration, cleansing power, and Saturation cost.[/*]
[*]Alchemy mastery and improved Cauldrons increase quality rolls, while impurities can create Pill Dregs.[/*]
[*]Improved the visibility and growth-stage presentation of spiritual herbs.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Interface, Balance, and Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added dedicated Sect, Formation Core, Relay, and Array Formation Path interfaces.[/*]
[*]Updated the Cultivator's Manual with Sect, pill quality, permanent Formation, upgrade, and Relay information.[/*]
[*]Added new Formation and Sect items, materials, sprites, projectiles, buffs, and visual effects.[/*]
[*]Improved NPC movement direction and several projectile behaviors.[/*]
[*]Updated English, Italian, and Simplified Chinese localization.[/*]
[/list]
