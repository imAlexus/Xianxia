[h1]Xianxia v0.1.3 - Spirit Beasts[/h1]

Spirit Beasts have entered the world of Terraria! Their strength, cultivation Stage, and rewards increase as you travel farther from the world spawn.

[h2]Spirit Beasts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added the Spirit Hare, a timid Mortal beast.[/*]
[*]Added the Jade Horn Deer, a charging Qi Gathering beast.[/*]
[*]Added the Flame-Tailed Fox, a Foundation Establishment beast that uses spiritual flames.[/*]
[*]Added the Thunderclaw Tiger, a Core Formation beast that attacks with leaps and lightning.[/*]
[*]Added animated sprites with improved ground alignment and movement direction.[/*]
[*]Added readable nameplates displaying the beast's name, Realm, Stage, and relative danger.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Distance-Based Progression[/h2]
[list]
[*]Stronger Spirit Beasts appear progressively farther from the world spawn.[/*]
[*]Their average Stage increases with distance, reaching maximum scaling at approximately 1,200 blocks.[/*]
[*]Distance progression uses the shared world spawn for consistent multiplayer behavior.[/*]
[*]Spirit Beasts can appear in forests, snow biomes, jungles, and beaches.[/*]
[*]Natural spawning, distance scaling, and spawn rate can be configured.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]New Materials and Alchemy[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added Spirit Fur and Spirit Beast Blood.[/*]
[*]Added Jade Antler, Flame Essence, and Thunder Essence.[/*]
[*]Added Beast Cores for multiple cultivation Realms.[/*]
[*]Added new Spirit Beast pills crafted at the Alchemy Cauldron.[/*]
[*]Added unique textures for every new material and drop.[/*]
[/list]

[h2]Debugging and Improvements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added [b]/xiadebug beast <hare|deer|fox|tiger>[/b] to summon Spirit Beasts for testing.[/*]
[*]Added [b]/xiadebug spawncheck[/b] to explain which Spirit Beasts can spawn in the current area.[/*]
[*]The spawn check reports biome, time, distance, Realm requirements, safe zones, invasions, and configuration status.[/*]
[*]Fixed Qi Flight being interrupted when opening chat to enter commands.[/*]
[*]Updated the Cultivator's Manual in English and Italian.[/*]
[*]Improved Spirit Beast labels with larger text, a dark background, and Realm-colored borders.[/*]
[/list]

Thank you for playing Xianxia. Spirit Beasts, balance, rewards, and regional variety will continue to expand in future updates.
